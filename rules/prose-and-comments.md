# Writing: prose, comments, messages

For anything Aditya sends or reads: chat, PR reviews and bodies, tickets, code
comments, reports.

## Length

- Write the terse version FIRST. Do not draft long and trim.
- A code comment states what the code cannot: the trap, the constraint, the
  reason. Five lines is a lot.
- Justification goes in the PR body, not the file: *"trim the comments and update
  the PR body instead."*
- One consolidated message, never a series.

## Voice

- No em dashes, no tidy aphorisms; they are what read as machine-written. At most
  one emoji.
- Humble and kind, and more respectful than a terse draft defaults to. Ask rather
  than assert where the reader may know better.
- Never characterise the reader or their workload.
- Never name a person as a blocker. State the dependency as a risk.
- Never record whether someone has left an organization.

## The reader

- A non-technical reader gets a different message, not a preface. Say what they
  must do or decide.
- Keep measured numbers separate from inferred ones.
- Every number carries its source in the sentence that claims it. Expect *"where
  did you measure this?"*
